Downtime, rework, material flow and scheduled stops

MTBF uses accumulated processing time. Failed equipment waits for a shared technician, then resumes its remaining work. Failed quality checks enter bounded repair-and-recheck attempts; unsuccessful assemblies are scrapped. A scheduled pause stops processing while work waits in place.

Design priorities and limits

Demand is the first constraint. Weights rank equipment or priced capital, operator count and equipment footprint among the evaluated feasible designs. Unpriced equipment uses resource count as a proxy. This bounded search does not prove a global optimum or include installation economics, aisles or qualified safety spacing.

    Engineering basis and data sources

    The frame sequence follows the X500 reference assembly. The extended route is a proposed manufacturing design; its timings and quality limits have not been physically qualified. Bench completion does not establish flight qualification.

    AI-PRISM assembly-duration dataset supplies the optional normalized manual-work samples. It concerns appliance testing, so transferring its variability is an explicit analog assumption. Raw drone times and reliability are not supplied by that dataset.

    Scheduling reuses SimScript. Downtime and shared-repair behavior follow the resource-and-interruption approach documented in SimPy's machine-shop example. Failure and repair parameters remain scenario inputs.

    About the assembly cell

    I built this simulation to work through how an X500 drone frame goes together and how many frames a team could finish in a shift.

    Explore components shows what each part does and where it enters the station. Start cycle runs one frame through loading, wire routing, fastening and inspection. Try a fault to see what stops the cell and what it takes to restart.

    Frame-cell capacity compares duplicate cells. Assembly line connects preparation, frame assembly, avionics, bench checks and final inspection. Change task times, staffing, parallel resources, downtime, rework and material flow to compare production designs.

    Product characteristics define the required checks. The scheduling model predicts consequences under the entered assumptions. Public benchmark data is identified separately; factory performance and physical equipment remain unvalidated.
