    About the assembly cell

    I built this simulation to work through how an X500 drone frame goes together and how many frames a team could finish in a shift.

    Explore components shows what each part does and where it enters the station. Start cycle runs one frame through loading, wire routing, fastening and inspection. Try a fault to see what stops the cell and what it takes to restart.

    Shift planning compares cell layouts and staffing against a production target. It shows output and time spent waiting for an operator. Task times are assumptions, and shift estimates exclude faults.
