    Simulation assumptions

    Each shift starts empty. A scheduled release is skipped when the kitting queue is full. Queue deltas compare against the baseline. The nominal capacity limit ranks the assumed load per frame and ideal stage capacity. The run accounts for queue blocking, contention and the shift boundary. Queues count waiting frames and have the same capacity at every stage. Kitting, prep, manual cell operations and rework share operators. Outgoing inspection has a dedicated modeled resource. Rework includes a second check and returns accepted frames to output. Queue and output estimates depend on assumed times and reject rates.
